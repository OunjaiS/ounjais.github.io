Flagship AI Projects

Cross-Retailer Promotion Price Comparison

Production · Vision + NLP + Multimodal LLM

2026

  • Five-stage retrieve-then-rerank pipeline (acquisition → promotion detection → text extraction → embedding retrieval → multimodal LLM reranking) matching competitor promotions to equivalent in-house products.
  • Retriever reached Acc@1 80.7% and Recall@10 97.2%; multimodal LLM reranking lifted end-to-end Acc@1 to 95.7%.
  • Abstention mechanism at a 98.0% true-negative rate with zero false rejections, so unmatched promotions are flagged rather than mismatched.
  • Manuscript being finalised for submission to Scientific Reports.

Product-on-Shelf Compliance

Production · Computer Vision

2026

Thai-Language Document Retrieval for Government Forms

Proof of Concept · Vision-Language Retrieval

2026

  • Compared OCR-based and OCR-free retrieval on a benchmark of 84 real documents (7 types, 111 queries), with 10,000-iteration paired bootstrap confidence intervals on every comparison.
  • Adapted a 2B-parameter late-interaction retriever (ColQwen2) to Thai with LoRA on one 8 GB laptop GPU, fully offline: nDCG@10 rose from 0.482 to 0.923 (+44.1 points, 95% CI +35.4 to +52.8).
  • Recommended an OCR-plus-lexical route for deployment (nDCG@10 = 0.981, no GPU at serving time), delivered as a technical report and executive brief with cost models.
