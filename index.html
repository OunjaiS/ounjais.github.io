Sarawut Ounjai

Last updated: October 2026

I am an Applied AI Scientist with a PhD from Imperial College London, building vision-language, multimodal retrieval and LLM-based systems that solve real operational problems. I take AI work from problem framing through experimentation to a validated, documented system, and I set the evaluation standard that decides whether a result is real: held-out benchmarks, bootstrap confidence intervals, ablation studies and error analysis.

As a Data Scientist at the Data Science & Data Engineering Unit, Gosoft (Thailand) Co., Ltd. (CP ALL Group), I set the technical direction for AI initiatives serving one of Southeast Asia's largest retail networks, spanning shelf-compliance vision, cross-retailer price intelligence with multimodal LLMs, LLM-based quality assurance, CCTV analytics and Thai-language document retrieval. I package this work so other teams can adopt it, translate cost and accuracy trade-offs for executives, and coach engineering students and interns in rigorous, reproducible practice.

Alongside industry work, I am a part-time postdoctoral researcher with Naresuan University and the Natural History Museum, London, developing a beetle classification model for the SITE-100 programme. My background in Life Sciences, from DNA barcoding and eDNA monitoring to phylogenetics, shapes how I approach data, evidence and evaluation.

Signature Results

  • Cross-retailer promotion matching with multimodal LLM reranking: 95.7% end-to-end top-1 accuracy with zero false rejections.
  • Shelf-compliance vision at 97.3% detection recall and 94.4% top-1 SKU identification.
  • Adapted a 2B-parameter vision-language retriever to Thai documents with LoRA, fully offline: nDCG@10 0.482 → 0.923 on a single 8 GB GPU.
  • Built a local-first, 9-agent AI system for data science work, with review loops and human approval gates.
  • 23 planned deliverables closed on schedule in H1/2026, each with a written evaluation report.
