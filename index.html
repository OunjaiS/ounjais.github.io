Sarawut Ounjai

Last updated: January 2026

I am a Data Scientist at Data Science & Data Engineering Unit, Gosoft (Thailand) Co., Ltd., where I develop machine learning solutions and data-driven systems for enterprise applications. My expertise spans predictive analytics, statistical modeling, and AI algorithm implementation.

Prior to industry, I completed a Ph.D. in Life Sciences at Imperial College London (2025), where I developed high-precision ML models achieving 99.98% accuracy in automated species authentication and processed over 170,000+ samples using custom bioinformatics pipelines. I also built computer vision algorithms for image-based identification and created automated data processing systems that reduced analysis time from years to hours.

My work bridges Data Science and Life Sciences, applying machine learning, deep learning, and big data analytics to solve complex real-world problems. I'm passionate about translating data into actionable insights that drive evidence-based decision-making across industries—from environmental monitoring and healthcare to financial services and technology.
